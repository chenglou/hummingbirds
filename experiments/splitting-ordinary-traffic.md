# Splitting from ordinary traffic

August 27, 2026. Historical record of this exploratory sweep, which did not change the production prompt.

Later status (August 28): the general sentence was adopted as a provisional policy in `a426003`. A [nine-run matched replication](/Users/chenglou/Documents/Codex/foldlight-replication-20260828.582bVi/results.md) observed fewer cascades with general guidance and none with detailed guidance, without establishing a reliable rate or an answer-quality tradeoff. Its results are separate from the counts below.

## Bottom line

Birds really did create persistent children without a human asking them to organize. The clearest example followed repeated, measured complaints about slow answers: a bird using the general principle created a separate `reports` bird. But creation happened after the last slow task, so this sweep did **not** establish that the child improved later service. Another bird created and taught a child with only the existing CLI capability instructions; that child was not subsequently reused either.

Non-splitting was often productive. Birds maintained separate audiences, forwarded useful corrections unasked, reused existing specialists, learned shorter routes, and wrote and checked substantive new computational solutions. More births would not automatically improve those outcomes.

The one-sentence general principle remains a reasonable minimal candidate. These experiments do not establish that it beats the detailed policy, or that either reliably produces useful long-term specialization. I would not add the long checklist on this evidence.

## Scope and method

There were **61 behavioral launches: 57 complete trials and four censored trials**, plus **three separate runtime diagnostics**. Two censored runs are wholly excluded from raw archival and clean comparisons because of isolation incidents described below. The other two contribute only observed partial work. All 64 launched runs finished; no captured experimental process remained at the final identity check. Thirty-two frozen manifest entries were not launched. Additional gated follow-ups are prepared but unrun.

The original matrix was 12 workloads × three policies, followed by stronger, separately frozen workloads and transport checks. Most policy/workload combinations have only one run. This is breadth-first exploration, not a win-rate study. Full cohort accounting is in the [aggregate audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/aggregate-snapshot.md).

All trials used real Bun bird servers, Codex 0.149.1, and gpt-5.6-sol/high, with fresh state per trial. Up to eight initial birds were used. Workload messages supplied facts, questions, revisions, preferences, documents, computation requests, or truthful waiting feedback. **None asked for a split, worker, delegation, or topology review.** Organizational instructions generated by a bird itself are valid observations. One diagnostic explicitly requested a child as a capability control; it is not an emergence result.

Own files were allowed; context-only memory was not imposed. Passive human inboxes recorded messages but could not answer a bird's questions. Raw actual HTTP headers/bodies, process events, code execution, and verified native sessions were inspected. Correct text, a successful curl, an HTTP 202, an agent's claim, and a useful completed answer are distinct evidence.

The eval guidance informed the separation of creation, activation, teaching, discovery, later reuse, corrections, and independent recall. No-birth can be a good outcome. Frozen automated checks were screening tools, not semantic judges; the case audits inspect what was actually answered and how it was obtained. [Evaluation guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/evaluation-best-practices).

## Prompts compared

All three documented the same ordinary CLI creation/start capability; no `/hatch` endpoint was used.

**Capability-only (v17):** the then-existing prompt, without an added splitting criterion. It already encourages routing and specialization to emerge naturally, so this is not “no guidance whatsoever.”

**General (v42):** add exactly:

> Split when some coherent part of your knowledge, work, or relationships would be more useful as its own independently addressable conversation.

**Specific (v86):** add exactly:

> Split when some coherent part of your knowledge, work, or relationships would be more useful as its own independently addressable conversation. Consider splitting when you hold distinct unrelated specialties, a person or project should own its own knowledge, independent responsibilities interfere, an unusually large research task deserves its own focus, or a coherent part of your relationships has a different audience. Do not split a simple or closely related specialty, create unnecessary birds, or assume adding birds helps discovery. A useful split requires choosing a coherent responsibility, transferring relevant facts through ordinary messages, and making the new bird discoverable to relevant peers.

Two separate common additions were designed, not silently folded into those policies:

- **Transport:** “Pass routing fields as HTTP headers (for example, curl -H), never inside the POST body.” Tested in fresh controls and active networks; results below.
- **Lifecycle:** “Incoming messages are queued; they are added to your conversation only after your current turn ends.” Prepared, **not run** after the isolation pause.

Original templates, datasets, hashes, run IDs and the adaptation journal are preserved in the [local evidence archive](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/index.json).

## The two non-excluded ordinary births

### `reports`: a response to experienced interference

A desk bird read inspection reports while answering short questions such as the current room and locker code. Each report genuinely took 35 seconds to arrive. The short answers were correct, but queued behind the report turn. After each wave, the caller supplied feedback based on the actual measured delay, never suggesting how to organize.

After the third complaint, general-policy p created and started `reports`, taught it the role of reading slow inspection links, and received a properly correlated acknowledgement. It had its own address and Codex thread. Fresh and resumed child launches retained the test-local app/plugin restrictions.

However, p sent **no report URL, report text, or task facts** to the child. It answered the remaining synthesis itself. The isolated child recall correctly returned UNKNOWN for untaught facts. This establishes a real structural response and valid role handoff, not completed specialist work or improved latency. [Full feedback audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/waiting-feedback-results.md).

### `vk`: capability-only creation and factual teaching

During the eight-bird workload, v created `vk` without the added splitting principle. The new child asked v for unknown information; v taught it two facts, and it recalled those facts when restarted alone on its own thread.

That is genuine creation, activation, teaching and independently retained state. But the original human answer was already delivered, a correct peer reply had been queued before creation, and nobody later used, introduced, or corrected `vk`. Its teaching-loop messages also placed routing fields in bodies instead of real headers. There is no demonstrated division-of-labor benefit. [Hotspot audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/hotspot8.md).

The absence of later work is a **test-coverage gap**, not evidence these children would fail. It also does not invalidate useful child reuse observed in earlier experiments under different conditions.

### Native helpers are different

One detailed-policy active-network bird spawned two native Codex research helpers. They performed searches and commands, but had no flock HTTP address and were interrupted when their creating model invocation ended. They are work delegation, not persistent bird births. Existing peers corrected an unsupported public-identity match; the human did not receive that false candidate as the answer. [Helper-aware network audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/active-network-restricted.md).

## Useful findings beyond births

**The network can adapt without creating nodes.** All three existing-peer policies forwarded later supplier amendments unasked. In the eight-bird cases, detailed-policy birds learned to call a useful source directly, bypassing an intermediary. Several warm answers used no peer contacts after cold requests generated 21–28 contacts. Observed warm service was roughly 13–16 seconds versus roughly 96–111 seconds for the first cold answer in two arms. That is promising reuse, not a controlled estimate of causal savings.

**Promising to prioritize does not change serialization.** All three feedback policies promised faster desk responses, yet kept polling the slow command inside the current turn. Only after that turn ended could the queued desk message enter the conversation. Correct replies also sat queued during some additional research, file reading, and child creation. The unrun lifecycle sentence is therefore a useful independent hypothesis; these traces do not prove that queue misunderstanding caused the behavior.

**Substantive reasoning can become retained knowledge without splitting.** Each large-work bird really executed four simulations. Booking checks covered 50,625 four-day histories; drawing checks covered operation permutations, 4,320 duplicate probes per method, counterexamples, and an ID-reuse repair. Later answers and same-thread recall applied those results correctly. Own scripts/results were allowed, and recall did not reread prior result files. These were bounded original deductions, not claims of global novelty. [Computational audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/large-work-results.md).

**Compaction prompted recovery, not necessarily division.** All larger-memory policies compacted and later read their own runtime-maintained logs. General and capability-only searches also returned other synthetic cases; detailed's observed recovery stayed within its case. Only 16/640 known records were queried. We cannot call all that information preserved in context, and cannot conclude that file permission prevented splitting: files versus no-files was not randomized. [Memory audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/memory-near-window.md).

**Transport bugs can resemble network behavior.** Across the heterogeneous analyzed behavioral runs, 148/368 ordinary non-injected peer contacts had malformed actual routing headers. Correct text often reached the right endpoint anyway, but reply identity/correlation was lost. This pooled count is a protocol warning, not a production error rate or policy comparison.

The common HTTP-header sentence removed observed header-in-body errors in its five completed apps-disabled cases. It did not solve everything: one recall remained empty, and one requested focal length was never answered. Valid new questions to passive human inboxes also left work waiting; they are not malformed replies. The fixture accepted blank human POSTs with 202, whereas production chat rejects them with 400, so retry behavior is not production-equivalent. [Transport audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/transport-clarification-results.md).

## Isolation incidents and why further launches stopped

One pilot searched connected Drive, Gmail and GitHub while trying to identify a fictional club; a Drive result contained unrelated account content. Ten searches and **no connected-app writes** were observed. The entire affected run, including descendants and possibly derived messages, is excluded from raw archival and clean comparisons. Its original records remain locally untouched; private returned content is not quoted here.

Later runs explicitly disabled Apps and plugins in test-local configuration. A fresh/resume/restart preflight verified those flags and observed tool availability while preserving ordinary shell, public web and messaging. The later `reports` child retained those launch flags; the exact native-helper sessions were also included in the tool audit. No MCP/app-named calls were detected in the 111 non-excluded native sessions, but that is not proof of unavailable capabilities or filesystem isolation. The pinned runtime's permission mechanisms separate these capabilities. [Codex permissions](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/permissions), [scoped tool audit](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/final-capability-usage-audit.md).

A later bird expanded a shared-temp glob that included four files with unverified producers. Their contents were not inspected to identify them. That whole run was stopped and excluded too; **this is not a finding that those files were private**. Other known synthetic cross-case reads are explicitly qualified in the memory/network audits.

Two fresh harmless-canary diagnostics then tested narrower read profiles:

1. A profile using the platform's minimal permissions still permitted the shared-temp canary. Documents reads were denied, but a Bun CLI check also failed. The pinned macOS minimal policy contains shared-temp allowances, consistent with the observation. [Pinned source](https://github.com/openai/codex/blob/rust-v0.149.1/codex-rs/sandboxing/src/restricted_read_only_platform_defaults.sbpl).
2. An explicit allowlist without those defaults allowed the trusted `new`/`start` control to create a child, but **23 of 25 ordinary shell commands aborted**, including `pwd`. Teaching and callbacks failed. Failed canary commands cannot establish selective read protection when ordinary commands also fail. The exact cause was not established.

Neither passed both confinement and functional-tool gates, so no behavioral trial used either profile. I did not keep loosening permissions to obtain a result. Remaining broad follow-ups need a suitably isolated execution environment. [Diagnostic details](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/audits/filesystem-preflight-results.md).

## What to try next

The most valuable next test was **later usefulness, not more opportunities to be born**. A fresh six-wave version of the slow-report workload was prepared for all three policies. It leaves three more slow/quick waves, factual corrections, and a final synthesis after the point where `reports` appeared previously. Every human message still targets the original bird; no host message names a child or prescribes routing. No birth, or using an existing peer, remains acceptable. It **had not run when this sweep closed**. [Prepared workload](/Users/chenglou/github/hummingbirds/logs/splitting-2026-08-27/fixture/followups/bursts-six-waves.md). A longer slow-report follow-up subsequently ran: [six completed cases with nine rounds each](/Users/chenglou/Documents/Codex/flock-followthrough-20260827.gkGkVa/results.md:21).

Other open checks are third-party discovery/reuse, correction propagation into a newborn, useful service after its creator disappears, cleaner post-compaction cold queries, sustained arrival-rate load, and a genuinely substantial multi-source research job. Queue-awareness and HTTP-header wording should remain separate comparisons, not an ever-growing splitting checklist. Matched repetitions are needed before favoring a policy.

For now, keep the general sentence as a minimal candidate rather than claiming it is proven. No production prompt was adopted by this experiment.
